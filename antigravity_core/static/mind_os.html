Mind OS Guide: What do these terms mean?

This operating system is designed to bypass emotional defense mechanisms and train structured, rational alignment.

1. Reality Check (CBT Experiment)
When you feel a sudden surge of suspicion, paranoia, or defensive reaction: write down the triggering event and your automatic negative thought. Force yourself to identify Cognitive Distortions, evaluate physical evidence, and rewrite a logical alternative. Finally, log physical verified outcomes to prove your brain wrong.
2. Common Cognitive Distortions
  • Mind Reading: Assuming you know what others are thinking without proof (e.g., "They laughed, so they hate me").
  • Personalization: Believing everything others do is a direct reaction to you (e.g., "She didn't text back because I did something wrong").
  • Catastrophizing: Expecting the worst-case scenario (e.g., "They are going to betray me and ruin my life").
  • Black & White Thinking: Viewing people as entirely good or entirely bad with no middle ground.
3. Preemptive Cutoff Risk (Relationship Radar)
Your baseline personality defense system is designed to run away (cutoff connections) to avoid betrayal. The Radar warns you if your **Leave Urge** exceeds verified, action-based **Trust**. When this alert triggers, you are in a defensive delusion. Do not act. Run a Reality Check.
4. Leadership Personality Transition
Leaders do not hide or run away. They communicate boundaries, ask clarifying questions instead of mind-reading, verify facts before concluding, and ground their nervous systems. This page is designed to systematically transition your mind from a defensive, isolated specialist to a secure, resilient, high-influence Leader.
